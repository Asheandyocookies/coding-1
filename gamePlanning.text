2.main thing a player needs to do to win the game. Because your game lets players pick between two sides, each side has a different main goal:
 Main Character Hero Goal: Explore the world, get stronger, and defeat the Main Villain for good by chipping away at their health across multiple fights.
 Main Villain Boss Goal: Keep killing the Main Character every time they return, while building up your power, looting villages, and recruiting helpers so you never run out of health.
3. The Main Villain: A giant boss with overpowered moves, huge health, and two different fighting phases.
 Deadly World Monsters: Dragons, wild predators, and dangerous traps hidden inside dungeons.   
  Villain Apprentices: Helpers recruited by the Villain who try to stop you before you reach the boss.
 Difficulty Penalties: Playing on harder settings takes away your E button survival tips and makes enemies tougher.
 Weak Sub-Mains: Your backup fighting style is less powerful, so you have to rely mostly on your main style.

2. Obstacles for the Main Villain (Boss)

 An Unstoppable Hero: The Main Character comes back to life forever and learns how you fight.
 Permanent Damage: Your health does when the Hero respawns, so every hit you take matters.
 Power Limits: You cannot pick infinite health or endless broken powers because of the boss point limit.
 Village Defenses: Guards, traps, or heroes trying to protect the villages you raid for loot.
